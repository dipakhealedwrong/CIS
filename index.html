Key space and security

The key square can be arranged in 25! ≈ 1.55 × 10²⁵ ways (about 2⁸⁴). At a billion keys per second, exhausting that space takes roughly 490 million years. But real keywords come from a tiny set of memorable words, and digraph statistics let an attacker skip brute force entirely, so Playfair is not secure today. Compare this with AES-128, which has 2¹²⁸ keys and no known structural shortcut.
